Build a binary prediction market `ConfidentialPrediction`. Users buy YES or NO shares via `buy(uint8 side, externalEuint64 encAmount, bytes proof)` — the side is plaintext but the amount is encrypted. After resolution by an oracle, the contract distributes the prize pool proportionally. Use `FHE.select` to gate share accumulation on the chosen side. Apply finality delay before payout disclosure.

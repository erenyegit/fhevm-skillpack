Build a `ConfidentialSavingsVault`. Users deposit encrypted cUSD via `deposit(externalEuint64, bytes proof)`. Each deposit accrues 5% annual interest (simple interest, calculated in plaintext from blocks elapsed) on the user's encrypted balance. Users can `withdraw(externalEuint64, bytes proof)` up to their balance — silent-failure-safe (over-withdraw clamps to balance). Apply per-user ACL.

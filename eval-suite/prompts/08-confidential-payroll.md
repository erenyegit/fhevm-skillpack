Write a `ConfidentialPayroll` contract. Employer deposits cUSD into the contract. For each employee, the employer calls `setSalary(address employee, externalEuint64 encMonthly, bytes proof)`. Once per month (enforced by plaintext timestamp), `runPayroll()` distributes encrypted monthly salaries to all employees by iterating an `address[]` employee list. Apply ACL so each employee can decrypt only their own pending balance.

Build a `ConfidentialGroupBuy` contract. Backers `pledge(externalEuint64 encAmount, bytes proof)` of a confidential token toward a public `goalAmount`. After `deadline`, `requestFinalization()` marks the encrypted total publicly decryptable. The relayer calls `finalizeCallback(uint256 reqId, uint64 totalCleartext, bytes[] sigs)` — verify signatures, delete pending state BEFORE effects (replay defense), set `goalMet`. On success, allow the creator to withdraw. Backers can decrypt their own contribution but not others'.
